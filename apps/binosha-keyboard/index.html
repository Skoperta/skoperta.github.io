Binosha Keyboard · iPhone

Write in Persian and English, privately.

Binosha is a Persian (Farsi) and English keyboard for people who write in both languages every day. It learns how you write, fixes typos as you go and types what you say, all on your iPhone. Nothing you type ever leaves it.

  • A full Persian layout with every letter on its own key, and a complete English layout.
  • Switch language with the language key, or with a short hold on the emoji key.
  • A number row, Persian and Latin digits, a half-space (ZWNJ) key and Persian punctuation built in.

Download on the App Store See what it does

Free, with no in-app purchases. For iPhone with iOS 16 or later.

What's inside

Suggestions that learn

  • Word completion and next-word suggestions in Persian and English.
  • Typo correction that understands neighbouring keys, doubled letters and Persian letters that sound alike, such as س ص ث and ز ذ ض ظ.
  • Learns the words and phrases you use, on your iPhone only.
  • Undo any correction with one tap of delete, and it won't be made again.

Voice typing on your iPhone

  • Speak in Persian or English and watch the text appear in any app.
  • Speech is turned into text on your iPhone by an on-device model. Audio is never uploaded.
  • Choose a standard model (216 MB) or a more accurate one (632 MB). Download it once, and delete it any time.

Swipe, emoji and clipboard

  • Swipe typing in both languages.
  • A full emoji keyboard, with the ones you use most kept close.
  • Your last five copied items, text or images, one tap away.
  • A pointing-stick style cursor control inside the space bar.

Make it yours

Light, dark and flat themes, including themes that follow your iPhone's appearance. Turn suggestions and correction on or off, and use adaptive key areas that make the likely next letter easier to hit.

Hold a key for its other forms: آ أ إ, ئ ي, ۀ ة, accented Latin letters and more.

And also

Works without Full Access

Typing, suggestions and correction all work as soon as you add the keyboard. "Allow Full Access" is only needed for the clipboard, key haptics and voice typing.

Careful where it counts

Nothing is learned in password, email, web address, number or one-time-code fields, and you can turn learning off or erase it in the keyboard's settings.

Private by design

No account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. What you type, what the keyboard learns, what you copy and what you say stay on your iPhone. Read the privacy policy.
