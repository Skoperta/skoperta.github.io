GoOrWait · iPhone

Leave now, or wait?

GoOrWait is a tiny departure board for the commute you make every day. Save each leg of your journey once: your stop or station, your line or bus routes, your direction and how long it takes you to walk there. Open the app and your answer is already on screen.

Download on the App Store See what it does

For iPhone. Covers the Tube, DLR, London Overground, Elizabeth line and London Buses.

What's inside

Only what you can catch

GoOrWait shows only departures you can actually make. If the station is a 6 minute walk away, the train leaving in 2 minutes isn't on your board. You see "Leave in 4 min for the 08:14" instead.

Is my route working?

Line status sits on every slot in plain words. If your usual route has a problem, your fallback moves to the top.

Honest about its data

Every board shows how old its times are. No signal on the platform? You get your last good board with its age, never old times dressed up as live. Live predictions and timetable times are always labelled.

Made for the street

One screen, no menus

Up to 5 saved slots across the Tube, DLR, London Overground, Elizabeth line and London Buses.

Easy to read

Big numbers you can read in sunlight, plus a plain "readable board" mode. Works with VoiceOver, Dynamic Type and Reduce Motion.

Private by default

No account, no ads, no tracking. Location is optional, and GoOrWait works fully without it. Read the privacy policy.
