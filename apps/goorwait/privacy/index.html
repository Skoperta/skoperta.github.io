GoOrWait

Privacy policy

Last updated November 2026

GoOrWait does not collect any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no tracking and no analytics. Your saved journeys and settings stay on your phone.

Who we are

GoOrWait is published by Skoperta Ltd, a company registered in England and Wales (company number 17570656), 71-75 Shelton Street, Covent Garden, London WC2H 9JQ, United Kingdom. Contact: dev@skoperta.com.

This policy covers the GoOrWait app only. For skoperta.com itself, see our website privacy notice.

In short

  • GoOrWait doesn't collect personal data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.
  • Your journeys stay on your phone. Your saved slots and settings are never sent to us.
  • Location is optional. GoOrWait works fully without it, and your location is never sent to us or stored.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Your saved slots: each stop or station, line or bus routes, direction and walking time you enter.
  • Your last good board, so GoOrWait can show it, with its age, when you have no signal.
  • Your settings, such as readable board mode.

None of this is sent to us. We cannot see it. Deleting GoOrWait deletes all of it from your iPhone.

Location

If you choose to allow location access, GoOrWait uses your location on your iPhone, only while you're using the app, to help you find nearby stops and stations. Your location is not stored and is not sent to us or to anyone else. You can turn location access off at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Location Services, and GoOrWait keeps working.

Internet

To show departures and line status, GoOrWait requests live data from Transport for London's open data service (TfL Unified API).

  • What the requests contain: the stops, stations and lines in your saved slots. They contain no account, name, advertising ID or location.
  • What TfL sees: like any website, TfL's servers receive the standard technical details of each request, such as your IP address and which stops or lines were asked about. TfL handles this as described in TfL's privacy policy.
  • What we do with it: nothing. These requests don't pass through us, and we receive no information about them.

Legal basis and your rights

Because the app does not collect personal data, we hold no personal data about you from using it, and there is nothing for us to share, sell or transfer. If you email us, we use your message and email address only to reply, on the basis of our legitimate interest in answering you, and keep it no longer than needed for that.

Under UK data protection law (the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018) you have rights to access, correct and erase personal data, and to object to its use. To use them, email dev@skoperta.com. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Children

GoOrWait is suitable for everyone and collects no personal data from anyone, children included.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page, with a new "last updated" date. If GoOrWait ever starts to collect personal data, we'll explain it here first, and ask you in the app before anything is collected.
