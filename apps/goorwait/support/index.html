GoOrWait

Support

GoOrWait is a tiny departure board for your daily commute in London. It shows only the departures you can actually catch, with line status in plain words.

Email support Common questions

How GoOrWait works

  • Slots: save up to 5 legs of your journey. Each slot is a stop or station, a line or bus routes, a direction and your walking time to get there.
  • Catchable departures: GoOrWait hides any departure leaving sooner than your walking time, and tells you when to leave for the next one, such as "Leave in 4 min for the 08:14".
  • Line status: every slot shows its line's status in plain words. If your usual route has a problem, your fallback slot moves to the top.
  • Data age: every board shows how old its times are. Live predictions and timetable times are always labelled.

GoOrWait covers the Tube, DLR, London Overground, Elizabeth line and London Buses.

Common questions

A train I can see on the platform board isn't in GoOrWait.
It's probably leaving sooner than your walking time, so GoOrWait thinks you can't catch it. Check the walking time on that slot and make it shorter if you're quicker than you set.
It says the times are a few minutes old.
You're offline or have a weak signal, so GoOrWait is showing your last good board with its age. It never shows old times as live. The board refreshes once you're back online.
What's the difference between live and timetable times?
Live times are TfL's predictions for actual vehicles. When no live prediction is available, GoOrWait may show the timetable instead, and labels it so you can tell.
Do I need to allow location?
No. Location is optional and GoOrWait works fully without it. You can change this any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Location Services.
The numbers are hard to read.
Try the readable board mode. GoOrWait also follows your Dynamic Type text size, and works with VoiceOver and Reduce Motion.
Does it work outside London, or with National Rail?
Not at the moment. GoOrWait covers the TfL services listed above.
Does GoOrWait track me or show ads?
No. See the GoOrWait privacy policy.
