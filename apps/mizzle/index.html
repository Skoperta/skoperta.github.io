Mizzle · iPhone and iPad

How green is your grid today?

Mizzle shows how clean the electricity is today in your part of Great Britain, as one friendly symbol, compared with what's normal for your region. Then, each evening, make your call on tomorrow's national figure: Over or Under?

Download on the App Store See what it does

Free, with no in-app purchases. For iPhone and iPad with iOS 26 or later. Covers Great Britain.

What's inside

Meet Dewey

Dewey is a little water droplet whose mood follows the grid. On a breezy, sunny day Dewey is delighted; on a heavy day Dewey is a bit glum, but never grumpy with you.

To get started, type the first half of your postcode. It's matched on your phone to one of Great Britain's 14 electricity regions, or you can pick your region from a list.

Today

  • A rating for your region from Heavy to Legendary, based on how today compares with the past year there.
  • An outlook for the coming days, as far as the official forecast reaches.
  • Clear labels showing how fresh the data is. Mizzle works offline with the last good data and never shows old figures as live.

Mizzle follows your light or dark appearance setting, and fits iPad windows of any size.

Tonight's call

  • Each evening Mizzle shows the forecast for tomorrow's national carbon intensity: the line.
  • Call it: will the real figure come in Over or Under?
  • Check back the next day for the verdict, and build your play and win streaks.

Just for fun: no money, no stakes and nothing to buy.

Widgets

Small, medium, large and extra-large Home Screen widgets, plus Lock Screen widgets. Make tonight's call straight from the widget without opening the app.

And also

The album

Rare and Legendary days in your region become collectable cards, if you opened Mizzle that day.

Backed by science

Every number has a small reference that opens its source. "How this works" explains carbon intensity, exactly how ratings and the line are worked out, and the limits of the data.

Private by design

No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no permission prompts. Your region, calls and album stay on your device. Read the privacy policy.
