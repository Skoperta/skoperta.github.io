Mizzle

Privacy policy

Last updated [TO FILL: date you publish]

Mizzle does not collect any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no tracking, no analytics and no third-party code. Your region, your calls, your album and your settings stay on your phone.

Who we are

Mizzle (the app and its widgets) is published by Skoperta Ltd, a company registered in England and Wales (company number 17570656), 71-75 Shelton Street, Covent Garden, London WC2H 9JQ, United Kingdom. Contact: dev@skoperta.com.

This policy covers the Mizzle app only. For skoperta.com itself, see our website privacy notice.

In short

  • Mizzle doesn't collect personal data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party SDKs.
  • Your choices stay on your phone. Your region, your calls, your album and your settings are never sent to us or anyone else.
  • Your postcode never leaves your phone. It's matched to a region on your device and then discarded.
  • Mizzle never asks for permission to use your location, photos, contacts, camera, notifications or anything else.

What stays on your iPhone

Mizzle stores the following only on your iPhone or iPad, where it's shared with Mizzle's own widgets on the same device:

  • Your region, for example "London". This is a region, not your postcode or location.
  • Your evening calls (Over or Under, the line and when you made them) and their results.
  • Your streaks and the album cards you've collected.
  • The days you opened Mizzle, used only to award album cards. Kept for 400 days.
  • Your settings, such as gentle haptics.
  • How much data Mizzle used each day, which it shows you in the app. Kept for 30 days.

None of this is sent to us or to anyone else. We cannot see it.

Your postcode

When you type the first half of your postcode, Mizzle looks it up in a table built into the app and finds your region. The postcode isn't stored and isn't sent anywhere. Only the region you choose is saved.

Internet

Mizzle downloads small public data files, such as today's grid figures for your region, from data.skoperta.com. That's the only server Mizzle contacts.

  • What the requests contain: no account, identifier, cookie, postcode or advertising ID. Mizzle keeps no cookies at all.
  • What the hosting provider sees: like any website, our hosting provider receives the standard technical details of each request: your IP address, the file requested, and basic device and network information. The file requested shows which region's figures were downloaded.
  • Who hosts it: data.skoperta.com is hosted by Cloudflare, Inc. on our behalf. [TO FILL: whether Cloudflare request logs are kept, and for how long.]
  • What we do with it: we don't use these details to identify you, and we don't combine them with anything else.

Mizzle checks for new figures when you open it. Its widgets refresh only when iOS allows them to. Mizzle doesn't poll in the background.

Links and sharing

  • Sources: source links open other websites (such as NESO or the ONS) in an in-app browser that you start. Those sites have their own privacy policies.
  • Sharing: when you share a card, it goes only where you send it. Mizzle never offers "Save to Photos" or "Assign to Contact", so it never needs access to your photos or contacts.

Where the figures come from

The grid figures are from the NESO Carbon Intensity API (CC BY 4.0). Mizzle is not affiliated with or endorsed by NESO. Postcode data contains OS data © Crown copyright and database right 2026, and Royal Mail data © Royal Mail copyright and database right 2026. Source: Office for National Statistics, licensed under the Open Government Licence v.3.0. None of these sources receives anything about you.

Legal basis and your rights

Because the app does not collect personal data, we hold no personal data about you from using it, and there is nothing for us to share, sell or transfer. If you email us, we use your message and email address only to reply, on the basis of our legitimate interest in answering you, and keep it no longer than needed for that.

Under UK data protection law (the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018) you have rights to access, correct and erase personal data, and to object to its use. To use them, email dev@skoperta.com. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Children

Mizzle is suitable for everyone and collects no personal data from anyone, children included.

Deleting your data

Deleting Mizzle deletes all of its data from your iPhone. We can't see or recover it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page, with a new "last updated" date. If Mizzle ever starts to collect personal data (for example with optional accounts in a future version), we'll explain it here first, and ask you in the app before anything is collected.
