Skoperta Ltd · London

Learning, technology and media, made to be understood.

Skoperta designs courses and learning experiences, builds software and data tools, and produces educational video and audio for organisations that want their people, products and ideas understood.

The name comes from the Italian scoperta: a discovery, something found.

What we do

Learning

Short courses, workshops and curriculum design for organisations and education providers, with a focus on data, digital and AI literacy for people who are not specialists.

Technology

Web and mobile applications, data analysis and measurement tools. We favour simple, well-documented systems that can be maintained long after we hand them over.

Media

Educational video, audio and written content, from explainer series to course materials, produced with a clear line between human authorship and machine assistance.

How we work

Human-authored
Where AI tools help us, a named person remains accountable for the result, and we say where they were used.
Built to last
Open standards, plain structures and documentation, so what we make can be maintained without us.
Private by default
This site sets no cookies and runs no trackers. Our products collect only what they need to work.
