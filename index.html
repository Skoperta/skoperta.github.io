Skoperta Ltd · London

Software, AI tools and learning for sustainable computing.

We work with organisations that want their systems to run efficiently, their people to understand the technology they use, and their ideas to travel well. We measure what we build, including its environmental footprint.

Talk to us What we do

The name comes from the Italian scoperta: a discovery, something found.

Why it matters

945 TWh

Projected global electricity use by data centres in 2030, more than double the 2024 figure and more than Japan consumes today. Source: IEA, Energy and AI.

Every system runs on powered, cooled hardware, and most of what it will consume is settled long before it is switched on: in the architecture, the model, the data pipeline and the defaults. Those are design decisions, and they can be measured. We help organisations make them deliberately, with the cost of each choice visible in money and in energy.

What we do

Technology

Web and mobile applications, data pipelines and measurement tools, built in the open where we can. We design for the long run: simple architectures, documented decisions, and an honest account of what each system costs to run, in money and in energy.

Learning

Courses, workshops and curriculum design for organisations and education providers on efficient computing and practical ways to cut digital carbon. Data, digital and AI literacy for people who are not specialists, including the judgement to know when a tool earns its place and when it does not.

Media

Explainers, course materials and short films that help technical and environmental ideas travel: accurate, plainly told, and labelled for how they were made. From a single briefing to a whole series.

How we work

Measured
Everything we build comes with an account of what it costs to run, in money and in energy, so the trade-offs are visible and the savings can be checked.
Built to last
Open standards, plain structures and documented decisions, so what we make can be maintained without us.
Human-authored
Where AI tools help us, a named person remains accountable for the result, and we say where they were used.
Private by default
This site sets no cookies and runs no trackers. Our products collect only what they need to work.
