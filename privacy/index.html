This website

This site sets no cookies and uses no analytics, advertising, embedded third-party content or forms. We collect nothing from you by visiting it.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc. GitHub may record visitor IP addresses in its server logs for security and legal-compliance purposes, as described in the GitHub privacy statement. We do not receive those logs.

When you email us

If you write to us, we keep your message, your contact details and our reply for as long as we need them to deal with your enquiry and to keep a record of our dealings with you. Our lawful basis is our legitimate interest in responding to you and running our business, or the steps needed before entering into a contract with you. We do not add you to any mailing list.

Our apps and products

Each app or product we publish has its own privacy notice, linked from the product itself and from its app store listing. That notice describes what the product collects, why, and for how long. Where a product notice and this notice differ, the product notice applies to that product.

Sharing

We do not sell personal data. Suppliers that process data on our behalf, such as our email and hosting providers, do so under contract and only on our instructions. Where a supplier stores data outside the United Kingdom, the transfer relies on UK adequacy regulations or the UK International Data Transfer Agreement or Addendum.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask us for a copy of the personal data we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, restrict or object to how we use it, and receive it in a portable format. Where we rely on your consent, you can withdraw it at any time. To exercise any of these rights, email dev@skoperta.com.

You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk, though we would welcome the chance to resolve any concern first.
